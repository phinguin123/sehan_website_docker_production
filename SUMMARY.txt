🔗 API ENDPOINTS
===============================================================================

OLD (Still Works):
  GET /api/reports/students/{student_id}
  Returns: Day-based PDF (Mon-Fri)

NEW:
  GET /api/reports/students/{student_id}/weekly?num_weeks=6
  Returns: Week-based PDF (Week 1-N)
  Parameters: num_weeks (4, 6, 8, 12)
